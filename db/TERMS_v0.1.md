# Ng'ombe: the terms, version 0.1 (English; the Tonga comes from a speaker)

Draft, 7 November 2026. Plain words, to be read aloud where needed. Not yet reviewed by anyone; Chris decides what stands.

1. **What this is.** Ng'ombe keeps records of cattle: who owns an animal, when it was born, what was done to it, where it has been. It is run by Zamai Solutions, the applied-AI work of Yumi Milling Ltd in Batoka, not for profit, for as long as it is used to promote smallholder cattle farming in Southern Province. No farmer pays.

2. **Your profile.** You give a name and a place. Your phone number is tied to your profile when you send a code from that number to the Zamai line, and from then on that number is you: a message from it counts as yours. Keep your phone safe. If you lose your number, tell us and we untie it.

3. **Your animals.** You enter your own animals and their pictures. You say an animal is yours; we do not check ownership, and the record is not a title. If an animal is owned by more than one person, enter it once and say so in the note.

4. **What you can see and change.** You see your own animals, pictures and entries. You can add an entry at any time. Entries are not changed or deleted once saved; if something is wrong, you add a correction, and both stay visible. You can download everything you entered, and you can ask us to close your profile.

5. **Who else sees what.** Nobody sees your animals by default. When you send a sick report to a vet, the vet sees that animal's record. If your animal is sold through the system, the buyer sees its record. The veterinary service sees counts and movements for its area, not your money. Where the law requires a report, for instance a suspected notifiable disease, that report is made.

6. **Pictures and the models.** Pictures of your animals may be used, without your name, to build and test models that read pictures of cattle, for the development of the Southern Province cattle sector. You can say no to this in your profile; your records still work.

7. **Your data and the law.** Your name, number and place are personal data under the Data Protection Act 2021. They are kept on a machine in Zambia, used only for what this page says, and not sold. Messages you send on WhatsApp pass through WhatsApp's servers under its own terms.

8. **No advice, no diagnosis.** The system sorts reports by urgency and sends them. It does not diagnose. A vet decides. Prices shown are what others reported, not a promise.

9. **Changes.** If these terms change, you are asked to accept the new version before you continue. The version you accepted and when is kept on your profile.

10. **Questions.** Write to the Zamai line, or to chris@zamai.pro.
